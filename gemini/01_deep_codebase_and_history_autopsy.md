# 01 · Deep Codebase & 83-Session History Autopsy

## 1. The Evolutionary Arc of `Sakhiya` (Sessions 1–83): How We Got Here

Tracing `STATUS.md`, `PROVENANCE.md`, `LEDGER.json`, and the `sakhiya/` module tree reveals six distinct eras in the repository's history:

| Era / Sessions | Architecture Family | Training Data & Teacher | What Happened & Why It Failed the Owner's Ear |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Era 1 (Sessions 1–29)**<br>`Wave 1–29` | FastPitch + Vocos (`docs/models/`) + external `sanskrit.onnx` (`77ethers/alpa` v0.2 4.5M) | 37h Microsoft Edge Neural TTS (`HemkalaNeural`, `SagarNeural`) + `77ethers/alpa` Sanskrit teacher | High UTMOS (`~2.85–3.11`) and low pCER because Nepali was distilled from commercial Edge-TTS and Sanskrit was literally `77ethers/alpa` v0.2 (`4.5M` Cepstral Source-Filter) wrapped in DSP. Properly withdrawn in Session 30 (`PROVENANCE.md`) due to non-commercial Edge data and unattributed Alpa weights. |
| **Era 2 (Sessions 30–52)**<br>`Sakhiya v2–v5` (`sakhiya/cfm.py`, `v2_vocoder.py`) | `10.26M` Matcha-style OT-CFM (`TextEncoder` + `DurFlow` + 12-block ConvNeXt `Velocity`) + `4.98M` `VocoderV2` (`NSF-iSTFTNet` + `Snake1d`, `n_fft=64, hop=16`) | `ai4bharat/Rasa` (Nepali + Sanskrit) voice-converted into a single female voice via 24-order Phoneme-kNN VC (`sakhiya/v5_vc_rsi.py`) + `SLR43` | Produced `v5_final` (= current `Sakhiya-V1`, `15.98 MB` INT8). Good prosodic pacing (`1.50` pauses/utt, `UTMOS 4.07`), **but** kNN-VC blurred consonant formants, `VocoderV2`'s 33-bin `n_fft=64` (`375 Hz` bin width) + `k=1..8` truncated harmonics had metallic upper-band comb buzz, and male voices were deleted in Session 30. |
| **Era 3 (Sessions 53–65)**<br>`v6–v15` (`v6_istft_tts.py`, `v6_nano.py`, `v15_rnd_wavehax.py`, `v15_pico.py`) | Zoo of compressed students (`Nano`, `Atom`, `Atom-Lite`, `Pico`, `Wavehax` sub-500KB vocoder) | 1-step deterministic residual SeqKD from `v5_c1` teacher | `1-step` deterministic CFM students (`t5`, `atom_lite`, `pico`) suffered severe over-smoothing (`UTMOS 1.59–2.94`); tiny neural vocoders (`voc144`, `Wavehax`, `450K` params) collapsed on high-frequency sibilants and glottal phase. |
| **Era 4 (Sessions 66–74)**<br>`Sakhiya-Pro v1–v4` (`scripts/sakhiya_pro/`) | `OmniVoice-600M` (`2.4 GB` bf16) fine-tuned on `1.94 h` (`SLR43 6834` + Seed-VC clips) -> distilled into `t30, t15, t10, t5, t1` | Synthetic corpus (`5.4–8.4 h`) generated by `OmniVoice-600M` (`Sakhiya-Pro v3`) | **Teacher-of-a-Teacher Cascade:** `OmniVoice-600M` had English/Hindi prosodic leaks (floating clause-end pitch slopes, inconsistent loanword reading) requiring best-of-9 seed rejection. Distilling a 4M student on synthetic outputs from a 600M model compounded both prosodic flatness and spectral haze. |
| **Era 5 (Sessions 75–78)**<br>`Sakhiya-One` & `Sakhiya-News` (`sakhiya_one.py`, `awp_vocoder.py`, `news_am_100mel.py`, `news_vocoder_100mel.py`) | `4.18M` Laddered Walsh-Hadamard + Monarch MeanFlow AM + `AWPVocoder` / `StudioVocoder100Mel` (`8.49–9.79 MB` INT8) | `16.13 h` corpus (`84.6%` Seed-VC converted + `15.4%` Pro-v3 synth) | **Seed-VC 16 kHz Ceiling + Homemade Vocoder Comb Buzz:** `Seed-VC` had a hard `7.6 kHz` rolloff (`HF_LSD = 10.75 dB`) and VC vocoder haze. Meanwhile, `StudioVocoder100Mel` injected stationary trig-identity sinusoids (`eps = sin(n*12.9898)*cos(n*43.7585)`) and `0.5*F0` subharmonics into `n_fft=64` bins, creating `3.48x` upper-band metallic comb grit ("karkar"). |
| **Era 6 (Sessions 79–83)**<br>`Studio-80M` & `cand_b_v2` (`studio_dit_clean.py`, `vocos_24k_adapter.py`, `train_cand_b_v2_breakthrough.py`) | `Studio-80M` (`107.8M` AM + `112.4M` BigVGAN-v2, staging) & **`cand_b_v2` (`4.12M` AM + `92.7K` Adapter + `13.53M` `Vocos-24kHz` = `23.01 MB` INT8 / `35.53 MB` FP16, Promoted Default)** | `cand_b_v2` trained on `1,425` clips (`85%` `Pro-v3` synthetic `wiki`/`sa_wiki` + Seed-VC `gita`, only `15%` non-`च/छ` real `anchor_6834`) | Swapping to `PretrainedVocos24kHz` eliminated the `n_fft=64` harmonic-source comb buzz (`grit = 1.39`), **but** left the tiny `4.12M` Monarch/Hadamard AM with a frozen duration predictor, bypassed/frozen F0 predictor (`p_enc=0`), zero frame-level attention, and unconditioned-F0 STFT harmonic blurring (`UTMOS22 = 2.64–2.81`, `0.20` pauses/utt, `+41%` jitter). |

## 2. Module-by-Module Codebase Audit (`sakhiya/`, `docs/`, `cpp/`, `video/`)

### 2.1 Core Python Package (`sakhiya/`)
- **Keep & Upgrade (Linguistic & Runtime Core):**
  - `sakhiya/symbols.py`: 64-symbol phoneme table (`PAD, WORD, BOS, EOS` + `PUNCT` + 36 `CONS` + 8 `VOW` + 8 `NASV`). Clean and compact, though `व` (`b` vs `w`) and `ज्ञ` (`gj`) must be disambiguated in G2P before symbol encoding.
  - `sakhiya/g2p.py` (`<-> docs/g2p.js`): Rule-based Devanagari G2P + morphology splitter + `G2P_LEX`. Currently suffers from 6 systematic phonological bugs (`EXP3`: `8.57%` word accuracy on non-lexicon words across those 6 categories), which we fix algorithmically in `g2p_v4_prototype` (`100.0%` word accuracy).
  - `sakhiya/numbers.py`, `sakhiya/translit.py`, `sakhiya/mixed.py`, `sakhiya/chandas.py`, `sakhiya/sanskrit.py`: Solid normalization, Roman-to-Devanagari transliteration, clause language routing, and Vedic chandas/svara prosody compilers.
  - `sakhiya/sanskrit_cepstral.py`: **Crucial historical artifact!** This 161-line file is the ONNX export wrapper for `77ethers/alpa` v0.2 (`4.5M` Cepstral Source-Filter). It already contains `analytic_cep` and `analytic_stft` (which eliminate 3.4 MB of static DFT constant matrices inside ONNX) and voice-conditioned (`is_f`) harmonic/noise crossover logic.
- **Archive / Retire (Superseded Acoustic & Vocoder Zoo):**
  - `sakhiya/sakhiya_one.py`, `sakhiya/news_am_100mel.py`, `sakhiya/v6_istft_tts.py`, `sakhiya/v6_nano.py`, `sakhiya/v6_distill.py`, `sakhiya/v15_pico.py`: Over-engineered compression wrappers (`WalshHadamardMonarchBlock`, `MidLayerPhonemeEngram`, `LoopedWeightTiedEncoder`, `SVD` convs) that crippled acoustic modeling capacity.
  - `sakhiya/v2_vocoder.py`, `sakhiya/awp_vocoder.py`, `sakhiya/news_vocoder_100mel.py`, `sakhiya/v15_rnd_wavehax.py`: Homemade `n_fft=64, hop=16` (`375 Hz` bin spacing) neural vocoders that caused chronic metallic comb-beating ("karkar").
  - `sakhiya/vocos_24k_adapter.py`: 13.53M-parameter `Vocos-24kHz` wrapper + `Mel80To100Adapter`. Consumes `76.65%` of the model's parameter budget just to invert mel spectrograms while still suffering from phase warble when fed harmonic-blurred mels.
