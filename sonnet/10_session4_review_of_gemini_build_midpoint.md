## 2. Verdict so far: **promising, not promotable. Three structural problems.**

### P1 (blocking) — the model is trained on 4.1 h of the 55 h we own
`/work/tts/s4/data/rasa_ne_voca/summary.json`: 2,621 aligned utterances, **4.11 h**, 2,493 train. The import (`--max-clips 3000`) ran in **52 s**, so full Rasa (31 K utts, 55 h; ≈20 h neutral/conv/news per voice) costs ≈ 10 min of GPU. With 7.5 % of the data a 4.5 M model will memorise its small text set; held-out texts (everything a user types) will be the weak point. Alignment quality is also modest: `mean_token_prob 0.724`, `mean_vowel_voiced 0.748` — worth a stricter filter (drop prob < 0.6) once the pool is 10× bigger.
**Ask:** run `prepare_rasa_voca_gpu.py --max-clips 31000` now (detached via `s4_progress.py launch`), keep the sentence-hash split, restart both trainers with `--resume` on the large cache. This is the single largest quality lever left and it is nearly free.

### P2 — trainer throughput is wasting the GPU
Per job 3.6–4.4 steps/s at batch 16 on a 4.5 M-param model; GPU shows 99 % but only 17 GB used. `sample_batch`/`collate_batch` run synchronously in the training loop, no DataLoader workers, no AMP/bf16, no `torch.compile`. Two 4.5–10 M jobs at batch 16 do not need the whole card.
**Ask:** batch 48–64 (+ lr scaled), bf16 autocast for the acoustic stack (keep STFT/GAN-D losses fp32), a background prefetch thread with pinned tensors. Expect 3–5× steps/hour → more *epochs of the big dataset* in the remaining ~9.5 h.

### P3 — loss shape: mel plateau after GAN onset
`mel` 0.58 (step 2.2 K) → 0.61 at GAN start (~3.3 K) → 0.568 at 10.5 K; `stft` 1.12–1.13 flat; `pitch` still falling (0.17 → 0.08), `pause_acc` 0.998, `spread_ratio` ≈ 0.99 (good — pitch dynamics are no longer flattened). The rise at GAN onset is expected; the **flat stft** says the vocoder side is waiting on the discriminator. Fine to continue, but do not read lower `mel` as "more natural" — the earlier default also had low mel and sounded buzzy.

## 3. Objective probe on rendered checkpoints (exp9)
Same demo sentences per voice (F: "नमस्ते, म सखिया हुँ…", M: "नमस्ते, म सागर हुँ…"), so F and M are not text-matched. Reference = REAL Rasa-F neutral from exp1 (`REAL_F`).

| Probe | REAL_F | t45 F @11 K | t10 F @9 K | t45 M @11 K | t10 M @9 K |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| f0 std (st) | 3.71 | 3.40 | 3.46 | 4.57 | 4.67 |
| f0 phrase std (st) | 2.90 | 1.92 | 2.25 | 3.56 | 3.97 |
| voiced frac | 0.893 | 0.966 | 0.979 | 0.877 | 0.913 |
| pauses ≥150 ms / utt | 1.73 | **0** | 1 | 4 | 2 |
| HNR (dB) | 16.7 | **16.1** | **15.7** | 12.4 | 11.9 |
| jitter (local) | 0.014 | **0.015** | **0.016** | 0.030 | 0.031 |
| tilt 4–8 k vs 0–2 k (dB) | −23.2 | −27.0 | −26.0 | −30.0 | −29.6 |

Reading:
* **F voice is already inside the real-speech envelope on HNR and jitter** (≤ 7 % off). That is far better than the earlier default (jitter +41 %). Median f0 is stable per speaker id across all checkpoints (F ≈ 229–242 Hz from step 1 K; M 190→151→139 Hz, still drifting down ~1 Hz per 1 K steps) — speaker conditioning works and the two voices are clearly separated.
* **F phrasing is too flat/unbroken**: phrase-level f0 std 1.9–2.3 vs 2.9 real, 0–1 pauses vs 1.7. The explicit pause predictor reports 0.998 accuracy, but it's trained at ~1.17 pauses/utt in the data and the demo sentence has a single comma-clause; a longer prompt with `।` and `,` would show more. Needs a text-matched test (below).
* **M voice is rough**: jitter 0.030 (2× real-F; typical male studio ≈ 0.010–0.020), HNR ≈ 12 dB, 4 dB darker tilt. Likely cause: male f0 (≈ 140 Hz) sits close to the fixed source-filter frame rate and the harmonic source has no jitter control. I do not yet have REAL_M reference numbers, so treat this as **suspect, not proven**. EXP10 (below) closes it.
* Darker tilt for both voices (−26…−30 vs −23 dB): top-octave energy is low → "muffled" risk. Expect the same "dull consonants" complaint as 24 kHz mel decoders; check /s/ /ʃ/ bursts on Listen.

### P4 — monitoring-tool bug found (mine) and fixed
`Progress.sample()` named files by whole-second timestamp. Gemini renders F and M within the same second, so **the M WAV overwrote the F WAV on the "Newest audio" card**: the F voice was never audible from the card. Fixed in `scripts/s4_progress.py` (nanosecond timestamp + speaker tag). Running trainers imported the old module, so the fix applies at the next restart (which P1 needs anyway). Until then Gemini's raw per-speaker files exist at `/work/tts/s4/checkpoints/<job>/samples/step_XXXXXX/*.wav` and exp9 reads those.
