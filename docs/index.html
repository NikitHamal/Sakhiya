About
A tiny, honest Nepali voice — what it is, what it isn’t yet.

  

    
flag
Where we are
The shipped default (Studio Vocos v2, 23 MB) is intelligible and fast, but not yet the natural, clean-accent voice we want. Session 4 is a full rebuild: pure studio Rasa Female + Male data, a 4.5 M-parameter two-voice model, Kathmandu pronunciation front-end, and blind-listening gates before anything is promoted.

music_note
Chandas · Laya · Svara
Vedic chant mode scans aksharas as laghu/guru, matches a metre (Gāyatrī, Anuṣṭubh, Triṣṭubh…) and shapes pitch with a three-tone svara contour over an optional tanpura drone.
