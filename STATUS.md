## Session 14 — box inspection, root-cause fix for the vocoder buzz (86.13 Hz frame-phase lock -> HiFi-GAN / phase-coherent vocoder), 37 h corpus training
- **Box inspection (turn 2)**: `sb-8b06583e5b11e3c0` alive (RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell 96 GB, 100% GPU util). Wave 14 (`am14` + `voc14` on the 37.1 h two-voice corpus `feats/edge.pt`: 18.68 h female `edge_f` + 18.38 h male `edge_m`) was in flight at 33.8 / 40 min (`am14` at step ~10,000+, mel L1 0.136; `voc14` at step ~6,000+).
- **Root-cause mathematical diagnosis of why Vocos (`sakhiya/vocoder.py`) plateaued at UTMOS 2.05 on GT mels (vs 3.39 teacher)**:
  1. `Vocoder` is a strictly shift-equivariant `ConvNeXt` operating at the mel frame rate ($f_s / \text{hop} = 22050 / 256 = 86.13\text{ Hz}$) with zero temporal upsampling and direct unconstrained phase prediction `ph` fed into `istft_same(n_fft=1024, hop=256)`.
  2. On any steady voiced vowel where adjacent mel frames are nearly identical, a shift-equivariant network outputs nearly identical `(real, imag)` complex frames at $t, t+1, t+2$, which `istft_same` overlap-adds as an identical waveform grain repeated every 256 samples — locking every vowel into an artificial **86.13 Hz periodicity / buzz** and causing destructive phase cancellation at every non-multiple of 86.13 Hz!
  3. Furthermore, applying nonlinear magnitude contrast (`log_mag * 1.22`) and EQ at inference time without phase reconstruction invalidates STFT consistency before `istft_same`.
